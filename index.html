🔐 Safety Tips

Simple habits can help you stay safer online.

🔍 Check Before You Click

Analyse unfamiliar links before opening or entering sensitive information.

🌐 Don't Trust HTTPS Alone

HTTPS protects the connection, but it does not automatically prove that a website is genuine.

🔑 Protect Sensitive Information

Never share passwords, OTPs or financial information with suspicious websites or messages.

📱 Be Careful With APK Files

Avoid installing applications from unknown sources and scan suspicious APK files first.
